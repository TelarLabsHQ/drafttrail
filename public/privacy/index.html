Privacy policy

DraftTrail is run by Telar Labs. Your writing and its recording never leave your device unless you share the file yourself, or your teacher uses the live in-class mode described below.

What DraftTrail stores

While you write, your text, your name, the assignment name and the recording of your edits are saved in your browser's storage on your device. When you tap Download, the same information is saved into a .drafttrail file on your device. We don't receive any of it.

Live in-class mode (with QuizPolice)

If your teacher sets up a live writing task, you write in DraftTrail inside QuizPolice. Your name, your essay, its recording, word counts, large pastes and times you left the page are then sent to QuizPolice so your teacher can see them during class and download them afterwards. This data is visible only to that teacher and is deleted automatically 30 days after the task was created. See the QuizPolice privacy policy.

Teachers

.drafttrail files opened on the review page are read inside the teacher's browser and are not uploaded.

Advertising

The information pages may show ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies; you can manage personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. The writing page and the review page never show ads.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across websites.

Children

Students of all ages may use DraftTrail. In the normal mode we don't collect personal information from them. In the live in-class mode we collect only what the teacher needs, as described above.
