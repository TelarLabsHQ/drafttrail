Set up a writing task

Choose how your students will write. Both are free, and students don't need accounts.

How will students write?
For papers and longer writing (optional) Students add their sources once, press “Cite” where a citation belongs, and get the footnotes or in-text citations and the bibliography made for them. They can download the paper as a Word file with real footnotes, and hand in the .drafttrail file with it.
Share this link with your students:
Open the review page
Ask students to hand in the .drafttrail file with their work, for example in Google Classroom.

Which should I choose?

  • On their own suits homework, take-home essays and schools with no reliable internet. It works offline, and nothing leaves the student's device until they hand in the file.
  • Live in class suits short answers and essays written during the lesson, when the rule is "no searching, no other pages". You watch the class live and collect all essays at once.

Neither mode can see a second phone or a book on the desk. It records when a student leaves the writing page on that device. For "no other gadgets", keep an eye on the room too.
