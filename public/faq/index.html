Frequently asked questions

Is DraftTrail free?

Yes, for students and teachers, with no accounts.

Where is my writing saved?

In your browser on your device while you write, and in the .drafttrail file when you download it. Nothing is uploaded to us, unless your teacher uses the live in-class mode with QuizPolice: then your essay is saved with the quiz and deleted after 30 days.

Can I continue later?

Yes. In the same browser, your draft and its recording are kept until you choose Start over. To switch computers, download your DraftTrail file, then on the other computer open "Continue on another computer" on the writing page and choose the file. The recording continues from where you stopped.

Can I use DraftTrail for term papers and case studies?

Yes. There is no word limit, you can write over many days, and your teacher can set a word goal and sections (for example Background, Problem, Analysis, Recommendation). The review shows each writing session with its date.

How do citations work?

Add each source once (book, chapter, journal article, newspaper, web page). Put the cursor where the citation belongs and press "Cite a source". DraftTrail writes a short code like [@smith2019, 45] and turns it into footnotes or in-text citations, with the bibliography, in Chicago (notes and bibliography or author-date), APA 7 or MLA 9. "Download Word (.docx)" gives you the paper with real footnotes. Always check the list against your teacher's guide: DraftTrail formats sources but can't check that they are real.

What if I paste a quote from a source?

DraftTrail asks whether the pasted text is a quote. If you choose the source, it adds quotation marks (or makes a block quote for long passages) and the citation, and your teacher sees it as a cited quote instead of copied text.

Can I write in Spanish, Filipino or other languages?

Yes. DraftTrail works with any language your keyboard can type, including accents and emoji.

What if I paste my own notes?

That's fine. It shows as pasted text, and your teacher can see it was your notes. Talk with your teacher about what's allowed.

Does DraftTrail detect AI writing?

Not directly. It shows how the text arrived: typed with pauses and corrections, or pasted in large blocks. That is often more useful than an AI detector, which can be wrong.

Do teachers need to upload student files?

No. Files open in the teacher's browser and are not uploaded.
