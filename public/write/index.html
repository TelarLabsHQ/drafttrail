How citing works

Add each source once. Then put the cursor where the citation belongs and press Cite a source. DraftTrail writes a short code like [@smith2019, 45] in your text, and turns it into a footnote or an in-text citation in the preview and the Word file.

The first footnote for a source is the full note; later ones are the short form. The notes, numbers and the bibliography update by themselves when you move text.

Check the finished list against your teacher's guide. DraftTrail formats sources but cannot check that they are real.
