Descriptive writing prompts

Descriptive writing paints a picture with words, so the reader can see, hear, smell, taste and feel what you describe. Tap Write this to open a prompt in DraftTrail and start writing.

Tips for descriptive writing

  • Use all five senses, not just sight.
  • Choose precise words: “sprinted” instead of “ran fast”.
  • Use comparisons (similes and metaphors) to make images vivid.
  • Move in an order: near to far, top to bottom, or the order you’d notice things.
  • Focus on a few strong details instead of listing everything.
