Opinion writing prompts

Opinion writing shares what you think about a topic and explains why, with reasons that support your view. Tap Write this to open a prompt in DraftTrail and start writing.

Tips for opinion writing

  • Use the OREO plan: Opinion, Reason, Example, Opinion restated.
  • Begin with a clear statement: “I believe…” or “In my opinion…”.
  • Use linking words: because, for example, also, as a result.
  • Give real examples from your own life, books or the news.
  • Finish by restating your opinion in new words.
