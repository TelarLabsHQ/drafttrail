Persuasive writing prompts

Persuasive writing tries to convince the reader to agree with you, or to do something, using reasons and evidence. Tap Write this to open a prompt in DraftTrail and start writing.

Tips for persuasive writing

  • State your position clearly in the first paragraph.
  • Give at least three reasons, each with an example or fact.
  • Answer the strongest argument on the other side, then explain why your view still holds.
  • Avoid fallacies like “everyone does it”. See our guide to spotting them.
  • End with a call to action: what should the reader do now?
