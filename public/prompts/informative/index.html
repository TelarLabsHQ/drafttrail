Informative writing prompts

Informative or expository writing explains a topic, a process or an idea clearly, using facts rather than opinions. Tap Write this to open a prompt in DraftTrail and start writing.

Tips for informative writing

  • Start with a clear main idea that tells the reader what they will learn.
  • Organize by steps, categories or cause and effect.
  • Use headings or topic sentences so the reader can follow along.
  • Define any word your reader might not know.
  • Check every fact, and say where it came from.
