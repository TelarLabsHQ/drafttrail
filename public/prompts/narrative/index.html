Narrative writing prompts

A narrative tells a story: real or imagined, with characters, a setting, a problem and how it turns out. Tap Write this to open a prompt in DraftTrail and start writing.

Tips for narrative writing

  • Start close to the action. Skip the alarm clock and begin when something happens.
  • Give your character a want and something in the way.
  • Show feelings through actions and dialogue instead of naming them.
  • Use time words (later that night, the next morning) to move the story along.
  • End with a change: what did the character learn, or how are things different now?
