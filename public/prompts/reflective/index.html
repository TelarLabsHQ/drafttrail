Reflective and journal writing prompts

Reflective writing looks back on an experience to understand what it meant and what you learned. Journal prompts are a quick, low-pressure way to build the writing habit. Tap Write this to open a prompt in DraftTrail and start writing.

Tips for reflective and journal writing

  • Be honest. Reflection works best when you write what you really think.
  • Describe what happened, then explain what it meant to you.
  • Ask yourself: what would I do differently next time?
  • Write regularly, even five minutes a day.
  • Reread old entries to see how your thinking has changed.
