What DraftTrail does

Records how the writing happened

Every keystroke, pause, deletion and paste inside the DraftTrail editor, with timestamps. Not the screen, not other apps.

Replay like a video

Teachers watch the essay being written from the first word, with pasted text highlighted in yellow.

Clear signals, not accusations

Flags for big pastes, very few corrections, unusual speed or lots of time away, each explained in plain words.

Nothing is uploaded

The recording stays on the student's device until they download the .drafttrail file and hand it in.

Works with Google Classroom

Students attach the .drafttrail file like any other file. Teachers open many files at once.

Free, no accounts

No sign-up for students or teachers, and it works on phones, tablets and computers.

How it works

  1. Student writes in DraftTrail

    Open the writing page, type the assignment and name, and write. It saves on the device as you go.

  2. Hand in the file

    Tap Download DraftTrail file and attach it with the writing, for example in Google Classroom.

  3. Teacher replays it

    Open the files on the review page to see flags, a timeline and a replay of each essay.

DraftTrail is a conversation starter

A big paste or very fast writing doesn't prove cheating. Students may paste their own notes or quotes. DraftTrail shows what happened so teachers can ask good questions, and gives honest students a simple way to show their effort.
